Rules & policy 24 June 2026

Buying from 1 July: a new identity check.

From 1 July 2026, Australia extends its anti-money-laundering law (AML/CTF) to real estate agents, conveyancers, lawyers and accountants. In short: the people who guide you through a purchase must now verify who their client is, much as a bank does when you open an account.

For buyers this means a few extra steps of paperwork: a passport or proof of identity, and for investors, evidence of where the funds come from. If you buy through a company or a trust, expect questions about the true owner behind it. For buyers, the check must be completed within 15 days of signing, or before settlement.

This is routine, not an obstacle. Buyers who come prepared barely notice it. We will tell you in advance exactly what to bring, so the process stays calm from the first conversation.

Source: AUSTRAC, June 2026. A plain-language summary, not legal advice. Exact requirements depend on your circumstances, so confirm with your agent or conveyancer. austrac.gov.au
