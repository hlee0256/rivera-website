The Residence

Grand in the city, calm as a place of your own.

In step with the energy of central Melbourne yet keeping a calm, private world of its own, the tower rises proudly over King Street as a contemporary architectural work by Elenberg Fraser. Designed across 65 graceful storeys, it reads like a sculptural piece at once soft and substantial, lifting the modern beauty of the city's western edge. Every detail has been finished with meticulous care. For now, only a very limited number of one and two-bedroom residences remain, ready for their new owner to simply move in.

Location does the quiet work here. King Street sits at the calm western edge of the CBD, steps from Flagstaff Gardens and the station, with the laneways and the river a short walk away. The tower keeps more than twenty amenities across five levels to itself: a hydro pool and spa, a gym, private dining, a library and a sky garden among them.
