The Residence

On the water, a place to come home to.

Aluna is the newest tower in Lendlease's Collins Wharf precinct, rising 28 levels right at the water's edge between the Yarra River and Victoria Harbour. Designed by Ewert Leaf and Studio Kennon, it carries a calm yet substantial presence, with floor-to-ceiling glass drawing in the light and the water beyond. The release brings studio, one, two and three-bedroom residences, sold off the plan and due for completion in 2029.

The real value sits in the location and the way of life. Collins Wharf is a riverside neighbourhood still taking shape, with a free tram at the door on Collins Street and Marvel Stadium, the Docklands District and Southern Cross Station all minutes away. The building keeps a full set of amenities for residents: a hotel-style lobby with concierge, an indoor pool, a gym, a spa, a co-working hub, a library, and two rooftop gardens on Levels 4 and 16 looking over the harbour and the city.

Rivera's view

Bought early on the water, with room to grow.

What you're buying here is a rare position right on the water, inside a riverside neighbourhood that's still filling in. The upside is the outlook, the amenity and an entry price that's still gentle next to the CBD core. The trade-off is time and scale: this is an off-the-plan purchase handing over in 2029, in a large tower, so resale will lean on how the whole Collins Wharf precinct matures. For a buyer who can wait and believes in this stretch of riverside Docklands, it's a sensible move.
