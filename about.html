About Rivera

A practice built on patience.

Rivera is one advisor, a short list of homes, and a promise to stay long after settlement. Here's who we are, how we work, and why families trust us with the biggest decision they make.

Our Story

It began with a simple frustration.

The best homes were too often sold like commodities: rushed through busy phone lines, passed between agents who had never set foot inside. Rivera was founded to do the opposite.

A practice that takes on only a handful of families at a time, sees every property in person, and stays in the relationship long after the keys change hands. No call centre, no churn. Just one advisor who knows your name and your timeline.

A boutique property practice · Melbourne
Our Philosophy

Unhurried, honest, entirely yours.

Choosing where your family lives is not a transaction to be rushed. We slow it down and tell you the truth, including when the right move is to wait.

  • 01

    We listen first

    A private conversation about your family, your timeline, and what home should feel like, before a single property is mentioned.

  • 02

    We curate, not flood

    A short shortlist. Each home seen in person and explained, with the risks named as plainly as the upside.

  • 03

    We stay, after the keys

    The relationship doesn't end at settlement. We're here for the renovations, the re-sales, and the next generation.
