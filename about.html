About Hà Anh Traco

Built on reliability.

Ha Anh Transport & Trading Co. is a Hải Phòng company that moves cargo by road and water, clears it through customs and stores it — all under one roof.

Hải Phòng · Since 2003
Our Story

It started with a single truck.

Hải Phòng is Vietnam's northern gateway — the port where the region's imports and exports change hands. Two decades ago, Hà Anh began carrying freight off those quays, one container at a time, with a simple rule: arrive when we said we would.

That reputation grew the company from road haulage into a full chain — inland waterway, customs clearance, handling and warehousing.
