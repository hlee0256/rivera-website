About Rivera

A practice built on patience.

Rivera is one advisor, a short list of homes, and a promise to stay long after settlement. Here's who we are, how we work, and why families trust us with the biggest decision they make.

The Founder

It began with a simple frustration.

The best homes were being sold like commodities — rushed through busy phone lines, handed between agents who'd never set foot inside. After eighteen years within Melbourne's major agencies, Elena Rivera left to build the opposite.

A practice that takes on only a handful of families at a time, sees every property in person, and stays in the relationship long after the keys change hands. No call centre. No churn. Just one person who knows your name and your timeline.

Elena Rivera
Founder & Principal Advisor
Our Philosophy

Unhurried, honest, entirely yours.
