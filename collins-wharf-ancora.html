The Residence

Where Collins Street meets the water.

Ancora is the newest chapter of Collins Wharf, the waterfront neighbourhood Lendlease is shaping in Docklands. A residential building at the western head of Collins Street, where the pace of the city softens beside the Yarra River and Victoria Harbour. Most homes are one, two and three-bedroom waterfront apartments, with a small number of premium townhomes and skyhomes.

The real value is in the location and the way of life. A walk along the wharf brings you to the free tram on Collins Street, Southern Cross Station, Marvel Stadium and The District Docklands. Designed by Warren and Mahoney, the building runs all-electric to a 5 Star Green Star standard, with a hotel-style concierge, a 25-metre indoor pool, a gym, two garden rooftop terraces and The Collins Room lounge overlooking Victoria Harbour.

Rivera's view

Bought off the plan, held for the long run.

What you are buying here is a waterfront address built by Lendlease and designed by Warren and Mahoney, in a neighbourhood still coming into its own. That means the name, the build quality and a dual-waterfront position that is hard to repeat. The trade-off is time and patience: this is off the plan, due for completion in 2027, and Docklands is still growing up around the wharf. For a buyer with a long horizon who is happy to wait, that is a fair trade.
