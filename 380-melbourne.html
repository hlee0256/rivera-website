The Residence

Quietly grand, in the heart of the city.

Two sculptural towers rising over Lonsdale Street, designed by Elenberg Fraser. A small number of two-bedroom residences remain, finished to a high standard and ready to move in.

Location is the real value here. Set in the very core of Melbourne, opposite Melbourne Central, a short walk from QV, the train station and countless places to eat and shop. The building has its own concierge, pool, gym and private cinema among many premium amenities, sharing its address with the voco by IHG hotel.
