The Residence

Endless views, at the top of Chapel Street.

At the northern end of Chapel Street, where South Yarra meets the Yarra River and the Domain parklands, 671 Chapel Street takes one of the area's last great view corridors. Bates Smart designed the residences to hold that outlook all day, from the green eastern suburbs at first light to the city skyline at sunset.

This is an off-the-plan release, completing in early 2028. One, two and three-bedroom residences and half-floor penthouses sit above a full wellness floor, finished with stone benchtops, timber floors and Gaggenau kitchens, with landscaping by Jack Merlo woven through the building.

Highlights

  • 01A rare northern-Chapel-Street address, between the Yarra River and the Domain parklands
  • 02Lasting views of the CBD skyline, the Yarra River and the eastern suburbs
  • 03Architecture and interiors by Bates Smart, landscape design by Jack Merlo
  • 04A full wellness floor: lap pool, spa, sauna, steam room, cold plunge and gym
  • 05Gaggenau kitchens and stone finishes, targeting a 5 Star Green Star rating
