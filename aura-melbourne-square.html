Chapter 01 · The building The idea

A vertical wellness ecosystem.

AURA is the defining gateway tower of Melbourne Square, OSK Property's masterplanned Southbank precinct, rising 67 levels around the 3,745 square metre Kennedy Park and its waterfall. Architecture by COX, interiors by DKO, landscape by Oculus. There are 673 residences, now selling, with construction from late 2026.

The design reads as a meditation on time: warm engineered timber, natural stone and earth tones, with a Japanese Genkan entry in every home. Body, mind and spirit rise through the tower as three amenity clubs, each its own state of mind.

Japanese calm, natural Australian beauty.

Rivera's view

Pre-construction now, built for the long run.

What you are buying is a brand-new home in a precinct that already works: the park, the supermarket and the first towers all stand today. The trade-off is time. AURA is selling now, with construction from late 2026, so it suits a buyer with a long horizon. For off-the-plan buyers, Victoria's stamp-duty concession runs to 20 October 2026, a window worth weighing, not a reason to rush. Deep Southbank rental demand underpins the income case.
