From the desk May 2026

The finest homes rarely reach the portals.

Some of the best homes we placed this season never appeared on a portal. They moved off-market — privately, between an owner ready to sell and a buyer already known to us.

Owners of considered homes often value discretion over a public campaign. A quiet sale spares them the open-home traffic, the speculation, the price talk — and rewards buyers who have done the work to be ready.

This is why we keep the relationship going long before you are buying. When the right home surfaces quietly, the people we know hear about it first.

A Rivera observation, not a statistic.
