Piccolo House

18 Barry Street, Kew
2 & 3 Bedroom Leafy Kew Wellness & concierge Garden pavilions
The Residence

An unhurried life, among the gardens of Kew.

Piccolo House is five low-rise buildings by Woods Bagot, tucked into one of Kew's quietest and most established corners. Rather than a single mass, the project is broken into finely scaled pavilions that rise gently from the garden and step back from the street. Each home keeps the proportions of a real house: three-metre ceilings, dual aspects and calm, generous rooms.

Location is the part you cannot replace. Minutes from Studley Park, Yarra Bend and the village life of Kew Junction, and ringed by some of Melbourne's most sought-after schools: MLC, Trinity, Carey, Xavier and Genazzano. An elevated, green corner that still sits right inside the neighbourhood.

Interiors are by Hecker Guthrie, the gardens by Acre, everything made to endure. Beneath the garden sits a full resort-style wellness level: a thermal pool, cold plunge, sauna, a gym opening to the garden, a wine cellar, a golf simulator, private dining and a guest suite for visiting family. A concierge is always on hand, discreet and attentive.
