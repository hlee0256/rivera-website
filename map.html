    Tram

    The Free Tram Zone covers the whole grid and Docklands. Rivera's CBD, Southbank and Docklands residences sit inside it or a short walk from its edge.

    Metro Tunnel

    Five new stations, Arden, Parkville, State Library, Town Hall and Anzac, link the university belt straight through the centre to the Domain, without a change in the City Loop.

    Southern Cross

    Regional trains and the SkyBus to Tullamarine leave from Southern Cross. 640 Bourke Street and 623 Collins Street are a few minutes' walk from its doors.
