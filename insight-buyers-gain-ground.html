By the numbers 24 June 2026

Melbourne values ease, buyers gain ground.

Melbourne's median dwelling value now sits near $812,621, around 2.9% below the cyclical peak set in November 2025. Values eased roughly 0.8% over the past month and 2.3% over the quarter, with houses (median near $958,361) softening a little faster than units (near $636,769).

Behind the numbers is a quieter market. Estimated sales are down about 14.2% on a year ago, one of the largest falls nationally, while listings have risen above average. More homes, fewer competing buyers: the balance has tilted toward those doing the buying.

For the right family this is an opening, not a warning. Quality homes in tightly held pockets still hold their value, but the wider softness means a considered buyer can negotiate without the pressure of a crowded room.

Source: Cotality (CoreLogic) Home Value Index and market commentary, June 2026. Indicative figures, current as of publication. Suburb-level numbers vary. cotality.com
